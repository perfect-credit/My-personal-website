## Decisions and trade-offs

- **Cold start.** How did you handle new users and new items? Everyone asks.
- **Offline vs. online metrics.** What did you optimize offline, and did it
  actually move the online number? If they diverged, that story is gold.
- **Latency budget.** What did serving cost you, and what did you sacrifice to
  hit it?
- **Feedback loops.** Did the system reinforce its own bias? What did you do
